Privacy Policy

BillJi POS & Digital Menu Management Solution

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Welcome to BillJi. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information collected through our mobile application, web POS system, and digital QR menu services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our order management and point-of-sale services:

  • Merchant Account Information: Name, business name, phone number, email address, PIN, GSTIN, and business address provided during account registration.
  • Order & Customer Data: Customer name, 10-digit mobile number, special order notes, items ordered, table numbers, payment mode (Cash, UPI, Part Payment), and transaction amounts for token generation and order fulfillment.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device identifiers and notification tokens collected via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to trigger real-time order notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly used for operational purposes:

  • To process takeaway, QR code, and dine-in table orders.
  • To generate sequential order tokens and display real-time order status updates.
  • To facilitate kitchen printing and digital receipt generation.
  • To send instant order status push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • To provide revenue analytics, daily reports, and order history to merchants.

3. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. Information is disclosed only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Secure infrastructure and cloud notification services (such as Google Firebase) that assist in delivering app functionality.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission and password hashing, to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. Your Rights & Data Retention

Merchants and users have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of their personal information and shop accounts. To request account deletion or data removal, please contact our support team at the details below.
